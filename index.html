Know before you park

  • Outside a CPZ's controlled hours, permit bays and single yellow lines are normally free — always check the sign on the street.
  • Double yellow lines mean no parking at any time.
  • Most of London is free on Sundays, but Westminster, the West End and some town centres have Sunday controls.
  • Zone boundaries and hours shown here are indicative — confirm on the borough's website (linked in each zone's popup).
